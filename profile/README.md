## یادگار · Yadgaar

**Sadqa-e-Jariyah, modernized.** Memorials where a loved one's memory stays connected to the
ongoing good they cared about.

- Urdu first, English alongside
- Privacy first: no ads, no tracking
- 0% platform cut on donations
- Open source (MIT)

A community project, made with ❤️ in Pakistan · [yadgaar.org](https://yadgaar.org) · contact@yadgaar.org
